How to read this. Each aircraft serviced (from the airline debrief forms) earns the station a set number of budgeted labor hours — the rates in the Service Budgets card. Facility budgets are a flat daily allowance.

Worked Hours = hours punched in Paylocity by hourly employees assigned to this station, plus 5.71 h/day (40 h ÷ 7) for each salaried employee. Hover or tap any Worked Hours number for its breakdown. Overnight shifts count toward the day the shift started. Holiday pay is excluded.

An * means the number is affected by data that isn’t in yet — nothing is ever estimated or invented. Hover or tap it for the reason. On Worked Hours it means a shift hadn’t been punched out when the file was pulled, so those hours are missing and the figure will rise. On Budgeted Hours it means AA hadn’t delivered that day’s debriefs, or Closeout Compare found a debrief problem: a duplicate debrief has been removed from the count, while a job on the closeout with no debrief is not added, so the count is understated until it is filed. Once the discrepancy is Closed the debrief is treated as correct and the asterisk goes away.

Under budget is good; over budget means the station used more labor than the aircraft mix earned. Days that haven’t finished yet are grayed out.
